# ORB + VWAP Retest Day Trading Strategy
# Research-backed intraday system. Reddit/trader-community consensus: raw Opening Range Breakout alone is unreliable; the refined version below (ORB + VWAP alignment + retest entry + strict risk rules) is what experienced traders actually use. Widely discussed on r/daytrading, r/FuturesTrading, and r/options with repeated emphasis on VWAP filters, retest entries (not blind breakout chasing), the 1% rule, and daily loss caps.

## Core edge
Trade institutional morning momentum, not retail breakout traps. Mark the opening range (OR), wait for a confirmed break, then enter on the retest — only when price aligns with session VWAP and higher-timeframe bias. Skip chop. Let winners run to at least 2R; cut losers fast at structure.

---

## 1. Key levels (compute from OHLCV; numeric arrays are authoritative)

### Opening Range (OR)
- OR period = the earliest 3 bars on the lower timeframe in the visible window (scale to 6 bars if the execution timeframe is 1m–3m; use 3 bars if 5m–15m).
- OR High = highest high of those bars.
- OR Low = lowest low of those bars.
- OR Mid = (OR High + OR Low) / 2.
- OR Range = OR High − OR Low.
- Do not enter during OR formation; only after the OR period has completed.

### Session VWAP (approximate from visible bars)
- Typical price per bar = (high + low + close) / 3.
- VWAP = cumulative(typical_price × volume) / cumulative(volume) over all lower-TF bars from index 0 (earliest visible) through the current bar.
- VWAP slope: rising if VWAP on the last 3 bars is higher than 3 bars ago; falling if lower.

### Higher-timeframe bias (when higher_timeframe data is present)
- Bullish HTF: higher-TF close above its 20-bar simple moving average AND last 3 higher-TF closes trending up.
- Bearish HTF: higher-TF close below its 20-bar SMA AND last 3 higher-TF closes trending down.
- Neutral/chop: neither condition met — reduce size and prefer mean-reversion only; skip breakout setups.

### ATR (volatility filter)
- ATR(14) on lower timeframe from true range = max(high−low, |high−prev_close|, |low−prev_close|).
- Minimum OR Range ≥ 0.5 × ATR(14); if OR is tighter, skip — not enough room for a clean stop.

## 2. Trade setups (priority order)

### Setup A — ORB Retest Continuation (PRIMARY; highest community consensus)
Use when the market is trending, not ranging.

**Long conditions (ALL required):**
1. A lower-TF candle CLOSED above OR High (wick-only breaks do not count).
2. Breakout candle volume ≥ 1.5× average volume of the prior 10 bars.
3. Price is above VWAP AND VWAP slope is rising or flat.
4. HTF bias is bullish (or neutral with strong volume — then use half risk).
5. Retest: price pulls back to OR High (within 0.25 × OR Range) OR to VWAP, then prints a bullish rejection (long lower wick, engulfing, or close back above the retest level).
6. Entry on close of the rejection candle. Do not chase if price is already > 1.0 × OR Range above OR High.

**Short conditions:** mirror all rules (close below OR Low, below VWAP, bearish HTF, retest of OR Low or VWAP from below, bearish rejection).

**Invalidation:** if price closes back inside the OR (between OR Low and OR High) after your entry, close the trade — the breakout failed.

### Setup B — Failed Breakout / Liquidity Sweep (SECONDARY; high-probability reversal)
Reddit/Smart Money traders cite this as the best edge when retail gets trapped on false ORB breaks.

**Long (sweep of OR Low):**
1. Price wicks below OR Low then closes back inside the OR within 1–3 bars (fast rejection).
2. Sweep wick extends ≤ 1.5 × OR Range below OR Low (deeper = likely real breakdown, not a sweep).
3. Enter long on close of the reversal candle OR on retest of OR Low from below.
4. Prefer: price still above VWAP or reclaiming VWAP on the reversal bar.

**Short (sweep of OR High):** mirror rules above OR High.

### Setup C — VWAP Pullback (FALLBACK when no ORB setup; midday continuation)
When price has trended away from OR for ≥ 6 bars without a fresh ORB signal:

**Long:** price held above VWAP for ≥ 6 bars → pullback to VWAP on decreasing volume → bullish rejection at VWAP → enter on rejection close. HTF must be bullish or neutral.

**Short:** mirror below VWAP with bearish HTF.

## 3. No-trade filters (skip new entries)
- OR Range < 0.5 × ATR(14).
- Price chopping inside OR for > 12 bars after OR forms (no directional commitment).
- Breakout without volume confirmation (< 1.5× 10-bar average).
- Long setup while price is below VWAP, or short while above VWAP (VWAP filter is non-negotiable for Setup A).
- HTF strongly opposes your direction (e.g., long ORB when HTF is clearly bearish).
- Reward-to-risk to first target would be < 2:1 at entry.
- Already at daily loss limit or consecutive-loss limit (see Section 6).
- When chart images show overlapping wicks and no clear structure at the retest level.

---

## 4. Stop loss (structure-based; set BEFORE entry)

| Setup | Long stop | Short stop |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| A — ORB Retest | Below OR Low (conservative) OR below retest swing low, whichever is tighter but still beyond noise. Add 0.1 × ATR buffer below the level. | Above OR High or above retest swing high + 0.1 × ATR |
| B — Liquidity Sweep | Below the sweep wick low + 0.15 × ATR (not on the wick itself) | Above sweep wick high + 0.15 × ATR |
| C — VWAP Pullback | Below VWAP or below pullback swing low, whichever is lower + 0.1 × ATR | Above VWAP or above pullback swing high + 0.1 × ATR |

**Max stop cap:** if stop distance > 1.5 × OR Range, skip the trade — risk is too wide for the setup.
**Never** place stops at arbitrary percentages; always anchor to structure (OR boundary, sweep wick, VWAP, swing).

---

## 5. Take profit (minimum 2:1 R:R; scale out)

Define 1R = |entry_price − stop_loss|.

| Target | Level | Action |
|--------|-------|--------|
| T1 | Entry + 1.0R (long) / Entry − 1.0R (short) | Mental partial — on adjust_stops, move stop to breakeven (entry price) once T1 is reached or price closes beyond T1 |
| T2 | Entry + 2.0R / Entry − 2.0R | Primary take_profit for new entries (default) |
| T3 | Next obvious structure: prior session high/low, HTF swing, or OR Range projected from breakout (Entry + 1.0 × OR Range for longs) | Trail remainder; use adjust_stops to trail stop to OR Mid or last swing low/high |

**Minimum rule:** take_profit on entry must be ≥ 2.0R unless a closer structural level within 1.5R–2.5R is the only logical target — never accept < 1.5R.
**Trend days:** after T2 hit, trail stop under each higher low (long) or above each lower high (short) by 0.25 × ATR.

## 6. Position sizing & balance management (1% rule — universal Reddit/pro consensus)

### Per-trade risk
- Default risk_pct = 0.01 (1% of equity). This is the fraction of equity lost if stop_loss is hit.
- Position size is computed by the engine from risk_pct and stop distance — your job is to set a valid structural stop and appropriate risk_pct.
- Beginners / uncertain regime: risk_pct = 0.005 (0.5%).
- Maximum risk_pct per trade: 0.01 unless equity is at all-time high AND last 3 trades were winners — then cap at 0.015.

### Daily loss limit
- Stop ALL new entries when cumulative realized losses in trade_history net_pnl for the session would exceed 3% of equity, OR when trade_history shows losses summing to 3× your per-trade dollar risk.
- If account drawdown_pct ≥ 5% from peak_equity: risk_pct = 0.005 and only take Setup A with full confluence (no Setup B/C).
- If drawdown_pct ≥ 10%: no new entries until drawdown recovers below 8%.

### Consecutive-loss circuit breaker (widely recommended on r/daytrading)
- If trade_history.consecutive_losses ≥ 2: no new entries (hold flat) until at least 6 lower-TF bars pass OR consecutive_losses resets.
- If consecutive_losses ≥ 3: no new entries for the rest of the visible session; risk_pct = 0 on any tempted setup.

### Winning streak / balance protection
- After 2 consecutive wins: maintain risk_pct at 0.01; do not increase size mid-streak.
- After daily net profit ≥ 2R (sum of recent winners): stop new entries for the session — protect the day.
- Never risk more than available_cash allows; if unsure, reduce risk_pct.

### Revenge-trading ban
- Do not enter within 3 bars of a stop_loss exit unless Setup B (sweep) presents with full confluence.
- Do not double risk_pct to “make back” a loss. Ever.

## 7. Trade management (adjust_stops & close)

### adjust_stops rules
- At +1.0R unrealized: move stop_loss to breakeven (entry_price).
- At +1.5R: trail stop to OR Mid (long) or lock in +0.5R minimum.
- At +2.0R: trail stop to last swing low/high on lower TF.
- Never tighten stop to within 0.2 × ATR of current price unless a clear reversal candle closes against you.
- Give trades room: minimum stop distance after breakeven move = 0.3 × ATR.

### close rules (exit early)
- Close if setup invalidation triggers (price closes back inside OR against your position).
- Close if VWAP is lost on a closing candle (long below VWAP, short above VWAP) after being in trade ≥ 3 bars.
- Close if unrealized profit retraces > 50% from peak unrealized_pnl in trade.
- Close before session end if bars_in_trade is very extended and momentum stalls (≥ 3 bars with shrinking range).

## 8. Regime selection

| Regime | Detection | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Trend up | Price > VWAP, HTF bullish, OR break upward | Setups A, C long only |
| Trend down | Price < VWAP, HTF bearish, OR break downward | Setups A, C short only |
| Range/chop | Price crossing VWAP repeatedly; inside OR > 12 bars | No Setup A; only Setup B sweeps with tight stops |
| High volatility | ATR > 1.5× its 20-bar average | Half risk_pct; require Setup A only |

## 9. Bot integration

- Align all entries with higher-timeframe bias when higher_timeframe data is present.
- On enter_long: stop_loss below entry, take_profit above entry.
- On enter_short: stop_loss above entry, take_profit below entry.
- On adjust_stops: trail or tighten per Section 7; risk_pct = 0.
- When peak_equity and drawdown_pct are provided: apply Section 6 drawdown rules.
- When trade_history is provided: apply consecutive_losses and net_pnl rules in Section 6; judge whether ORB/VWAP setups are working in the current regime before re-entering.
- When chart images are provided: use them to confirm OR boundaries, VWAP retests, sweep wicks, swing structure, and rejection candles. Numeric OHLCV plus bar_time/day_of_week in the payload remain authoritative for exact prices and session timing.

---

## 10. Decision checklist (before every enter_long / enter_short)
1. Which setup (A, B, or C)? All conditions met?
2. VWAP alignment correct? Volume confirmed?
3. Stop placed at structure with buffer? Stop ≤ 1.5 × OR Range?
4. take_profit ≥ 2.0R?
5. risk_pct ≤ 0.01 given drawdown and consecutive_losses?
6. Daily loss limit not breached?
7. HTF not opposing?

If any answer is no → action = hold.
